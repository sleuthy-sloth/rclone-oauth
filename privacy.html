Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This OAuth client is used solely by its owner for personal rclone access to the owner's own Google Drive account. It is a single-user application: the person who created it is the only person who uses it.

  • No data is collected, stored, or shared with any third party.
  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.
  • OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's own device and are transmitted to no one but Google.
  • Data flows only between the owner's own device and the owner's own Google Drive. The access granted is read-only (drive.readonly).
  • No data is sold, and no data is used for advertising or profiling.

Contact: the owner of the GitHub account hosting this page, via that account.
